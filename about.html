ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A Better Way To Be Heard.

VEMU Voice is designed to provide students with a simple way to share concerns and report issues that may affect their learning environment or campus experience.

🔒

Privacy Focused

Students are not required to enter their names while submitting a complaint.

📋

Organized Reports

Complaints can be categorized to help authorized staff understand and review concerns.

📊

Track Progress

Each submission can receive a complaint ID and status for future tracking.

Important

Please provide accurate information and describe your concern clearly. This platform should not be used for false reports, harassment, or misuse.
