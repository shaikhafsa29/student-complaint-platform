ABOUT VEMU VOICE

A better way to raise student concerns.

VEMU Voice is designed to make student complaints easier to submit, route and monitor.

📢

Raise Concerns

Students can submit concerns through a structured digital complaint form.

🎯

Right Authority

Students can select whether the complaint should be directed to the Class Teacher, HOD or Principal.

📊

Track Progress

Each complaint receives a unique ID that can be used to check its current status.

Important

Students do not have to enter their name in the complaint form. The system uses the student's Roll Number for access and complaint identification within the platform.
